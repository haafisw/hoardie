Privacy Policy

Hoardie keeps things for you. It doesn't keep anything about you.

In short

Hoardie has no account, no analytics, no ads and no trackers, and there is no Hoardie server. The links you save go from your iPhone to your own Mac, and the files go straight into your library.

What happens when you save something

When you paste a link and tap Save, the Hoardie app sends that link to Hoardie Helper, a small program running on your own Mac. The helper asks the platform for the public post, the same way a web browser would, and sends the file back to your iPhone over your local network.

A pairing code shown on your Mac keeps other devices on the same network from using your helper.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your history: the links you saved, the account names, small preview images, file sizes and dates.
  • Your settings: your Mac's address, the pairing code, where files are saved and whether haptics are on.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting Hoardie deletes it. The photos and videos you saved stay in Photos or Files until you delete them yourself.

What the platforms see

To fetch a post, Hoardie Helper makes an ordinary request from your Mac's internet connection to the platform that hosts it, such as Instagram or YouTube. That platform sees a normal request for a public page, as it would from any browser. Hoardie never signs in to your social accounts.

Permissions Hoardie asks for

  • Add to Photos, so saved files can go into your library. Hoardie can't read the photos already there.
  • Local Network, so your iPhone can find Hoardie Helper on your Mac.
  • Paste, when you tap the Hoardie button, so it can read the link you copied. It reads the clipboard only when you tap.

Children

Hoardie collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Hoardie won't start collecting personal data without asking you first.
