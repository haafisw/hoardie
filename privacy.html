Privacy Policy

Hoardie keeps things for you. It doesn't keep anything about you.

In short

Hoardie has no account, no analytics, no ads and no trackers, and there is no Hoardie server. Links are read on your iPhone (or on your own Mac, if you choose), and the files go straight into your library.

What happens when you save something

When you paste a link and tap Save, Hoardie asks the platform for that public post, the same way a web browser would, right from your iPhone. It downloads the original file and saves it to Photos or Files.

If you choose Read links on ▸ My Mac in Settings, the link goes to Hoardie Helper, a small program on your own Mac, which does the same and sends the file back over your local network. A pairing code shown on your Mac keeps other devices from using your helper.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your history: the links you saved, the account names, small preview images, file sizes and dates.
  • Your settings: where links are read, where files are saved, whether haptics are on, and your Mac's address and pairing code if you use Hoardie Helper.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting Hoardie deletes it. The photos and videos you saved stay in Photos or Files until you delete them yourself.

What the platforms see

To fetch a post, Hoardie makes an ordinary request from your iPhone's (or your Mac's) internet connection to the platform that hosts it, such as Instagram or YouTube. That platform sees a normal request for a public page, as it would from any browser. Hoardie never signs in to your social accounts.

Permissions Hoardie asks for

  • Add to Photos, so saved files can go into your library. Hoardie can't read the photos already there.
  • Local Network, only if you use Hoardie Helper, so your iPhone can find it on your Mac.
  • Paste, when you tap the Hoardie button, so it can read the link you copied. It reads the clipboard only when you tap.

Children

Hoardie collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Hoardie won't start collecting personal data without asking you first.
